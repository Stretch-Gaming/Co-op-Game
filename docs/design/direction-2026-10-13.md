# Design direction: the maze is the level

October 13, 2026 · Stretch's clarification

This supersedes the route and layout portions of the October 10 direction. Keep the actual Ak47/PK maze as the level, including both large original meetup halls. Do not replace a meetup hall with isolated tubes or attach a series of unrelated rooms to the adventure.

The normal starting room and its mining/cart/plate puzzle stay. Solving that puzzle opens the door into the maze. Beyond that door, traversal, puzzles and the relic return take place inside the maze's existing footprint. Remove the extra cave-return chambers and the outside carrier walkway from the playable assembly; preserve the collaborators' original source maps and art.

## The route

1. Start in the existing mining room and solve its puzzle to open the maze door.
2. Enter the maze, experience the taking, and follow the first colored paths to meetup hall A.
3. Solve hall A's shared light puzzle to open its onward paths. Follow the original middle paths to meetup hall B, regroup, and solve that hall's shared light puzzle to open the last paths.
4. Follow the original third paths to the relic chamber.
5. Hold the existing cage levers and take the relic.
6. Return through the maze to the starting-room drop-off, with live threats and teammate protection. Results and host restart remain.

Six rounds remain: mining; first maze; both meetup-hall progression stages; final maze; relic; escape. Hall A alone does not advance into the final maze. Hall B's arrival provides a regroup and retry point. The GameMode alone changes round state. Solved hall gates allow reverse travel during escape.

## Traversal variety

Each colored path needs distinct sequences of rocky narrows, rubble steps, flat-topped jump-up rocks, ledges, fair jump gaps, bridges and amber-marked climb sections. Build clusters of useful movements with short recovery/orientation stretches, not long empty corridors or repeated generic obstacles. Preserve the original over/under crossings, room footprints and the clear approaches to puzzle gates.

Use the existing movement measurements: walkable steps up to 45 cm, jump-up rocks below 1 m, mantle ledges about 1–2 m, short walk-jump gaps about 1.5–2.5 m, and sprint gaps about 3–4 m with at least 6 m clear run-up. Ordinary missed jumps lead to same-path recovery pits with a climb or stairs; they do not become unexplained lethal drops or shortcuts between stacked paths.

The heavy relic carrier still cannot sprint or climb. A visible safe line through existing corridors must support the slower return without compulsory jumps, climbs or teleportation. Riskier jump/climb options remain useful alongside that line. This is authored variety; do not claim unbounded procedural generation without a separate requested design.

## Acceptance

Verify normal spawn, mining and the entrance door; both restored meetup halls; owning-input puzzle progression and blocked bypasses; obstacle traversal and failed-jump recovery; retry/regroup before and after hall B; continuous heavy-carrier return, drop/re-pick and delivery; results and restart. Cover 2, 3 and 4 players and both host split-screen owners. Keep native scripted checks, rendered review, packaged startup and full human/device playtesting distinct.
